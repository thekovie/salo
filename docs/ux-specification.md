# UX specification

What is built, screen by screen. Design world and tokens: `PRODUCT.md`, the direction contract in `.impeccable/surfaces/src-app.md`, and `DESIGN.md` (written after the workspace shipped).

## Landing (`/`)
- Primary action **Start an order** (one tap: creates the session and opens the workspace). Errors appear under the button in plain words.
- **Pick up where you left off**: orders this browser is part of (from its session cookies), newest first, up to five.
- **Join with a code**: six characters; lower case, spaces, dashes and look-alikes (I, L → 1; O → 0; U → V) are forgiven; opens the invite page.
- **Hero scene** (labelled "Sample order"): a Round 1 ticket that fills itself on a loop. "Rafa added…" pill, counts merge, "No sauce" splits off, the total ticks, the CONFIRMED stamp lands.
- **How it works**, four rows (text with an illustrated phone scene that plays once when scrolled into view): start and invite (QR, code, people joining); everyone adds their own (typing, Add, counts merging with a "+2 · JD" tag); one ticket for the waiter (big numbers, stamp); settle up later (who ordered what, Copy text / Save image; the text is price-free, since prices and Copy summary live in the KKB tab). Step 3 also mentions tracking what's been served. The hero subline reads "Order together, keep track of what's been served, and settle everyone's share—all in one place." Scenes are decorative (`aria-hidden`); with reduced motion they show their last frame, still.
- "Why Salo" strip (no app, live on every phone with served food and in-app updates, rounds and fixes, gone after the meal), a closing **Start your table's order** ticket, and a footer link to How it works.
- **Setup guide** (in an order; replaces the spotlight tour): a saffron card "New to Salo? A short setup guide shows everything you can do here." (Start guide / Not now), once per browser and role. The guide is a full-screen wizard: "Step n of N", a tappable progress bar (green for steps already done in this order), a small moving picture per step, the title with a ✓ Done badge when it applies, what the feature is for, **Try it** (closes the guide and lands on the feature: opens Invite, focuses the dish field, opens the price panel, switches to People or Rounds, opens Review, the waiter ticket or KKB; disabled with the reason when it can't work yet, e.g. "Add a dish first"), Back and Next. Host, 9 steps: set up your table (your name and the order name, saved right there), invite, add dishes, who it's for / sizes / prices, People and Groups, review and confirm, add-ons and fixes, KKB, then "You're all set" with a checklist of what's done (tap one to jump back to it). Guest, 6 steps: add dishes, for someone / price, I'm ready, the ticket and That's mine, their KKB share, all set. Arrow keys move, Escape closes, focus moves to each step's heading. Replay: host from Order options → How Salo works, guest from the Help button in the header.

## Workspace (`/s/:id`)
- **Header band** (tomato), two rows so nothing truncates: ‹ back home, then the order name with "Round n · Open" and the sync pill (Saved shows as a check) under it. The second row holds Invite and, for the host, a labelled **Options ▾** button opening the Order options drawer (name, Cancel/End order); guests get a labelled **Help** button instead. Under 400 px the two buttons share the row in equal halves. After a round is confirmed the host also sees **What's next?** (Add more (round N) / End the order), and the Rounds tab has End the order next to Start round N. Sync pill: Saving… / Saved / Offline · not saved yet / Changed elsewhere / Not saved. Announced politely to screen readers.
- **Dock and floating notices:** the composer keeps every label at every width (under 360 px "For …" takes its own row and Add keeps its word). The "who's it for" panel closes after Add and on a tab change. A guest's I'm ready and Ticket split a row under 360 px. Toasts, the connection pill and "added" pills share one stack that sits just above the dock at its measured height (`--dock-h`), so nothing covers the composer or the top of the content.
- **Ticket**: consolidated dishes with big counts. A dish with one source entry shows a stepper directly; at 1 the minus becomes Remove. A dish with several entries shows its total and, if needed, its preparations ("Regular ×2", "No sauce ×1"); tapping the total opens the source entries, each with its own stepper and who it is for.
- **Empty state**: "Wala pang order. Type the first dish below."
- **Composer** (pinned at the bottom), two rows. Row one: a full-width **Dish** field with a visible label. Row two: **For <name> ▾** (who the next dish is for), the quantity stepper (starts at 1), **Add**. Suggestions appear above the field for dishes already in this order (starts-with first, then contains; up to four). The For button opens *Who's it for?*: one-tap chips built from the order — **Me** (default for host and guests), each other person in the order, names typed earlier for people without a phone, groups used so far, Whole table, Anyone, **+ Someone else**, **+ New group** (the last two show a name field; the typed name becomes a chip). Under the Dish field sits a row of three outline chips, **+ Size**, **+ Note** and **+ ₱ Price** (44 px, `aria-expanded`). A chip opens only its own field under the row and focuses it; one field is open at a time. Fields: **Size or variant**, **Instructions**, **Price each (₱)** (decimal keypad, optional, with "₱120.00 × 2 = ₱240.00" under it). A filled field collapses into a tag ("Large ✕", "No sauce ✕", "₱120.00 × 2 = ₱240.00 ✕"): tapping the tag reopens the field, ✕ clears it. When Size opens and the dish already has sizes in this order, up to four are offered as "used in this order" chips; tapping one fills Size. A dish typed with a comma ("Gyoza, large, no sauce") offers *Split into Gyoza · size “large” · note “no sauce”?* with **Split** and **Keep as typed**: the part after the first comma is the size if it is a size already used for that dish or a short size word (small, medium, large, regular, solo, sharing, family, hot, iced), otherwise a note; later parts join the note; never offered for a name already in this order; never applied on its own. An invalid price blocks Add with the reason; everything resets after Add and a suggestion never fills the price. When the typed dish and size already have a table price (set by the host in KKB) and Price is empty, the hint under the field reads "Table price ₱120.00. Leave blank to use it."; the composer never creates a table price. The For panel holds only the recipients and has a Close (X). Chips are 44 px tall.
- **Someone else adds a dish**: a small saffron pill under the header ("JD added Gyoza ×2"; several at once: "JD added 3 dishes") slides in, stays about 2.5 s and fades; tapping it jumps to the dish. The dish row gets a "+2 · JD" tag and the wash. Never for your own dishes; quiet on load and resync. Under reduced motion it fades only.
- **Removing a guest** (host, People tab): the remove icon on a guest's card opens a drawer with an optional reason (≤140, only that guest sees it) and what happens (their own items in the open round come off; they can join again). Toast "JD removed." with Undo. The removed guest's screen says "You're no longer in this order", quotes the reason, and offers **Join again** while the order is open.
- **Removing a dish keeps focus**: when the focused dish row goes away (stepper Remove, or another phone removed it), focus moves to the same control in the next row, else the previous row, else the Dish field. It never lands on a hidden, disabled or detached element.
- **Editing a draft dish**: a pencil on each line you may change opens "Edit dish" (Size and Note use the same chips and tags as the composer, no price; who it's for; the chips work with arrow keys). Quantity stays on the stepper. Quantity changes and edits show an Undo toast. The host also gets **Mark <dish> unavailable** for a dish that only exists in the open round: it removes every entry with a toast "Sisig marked unavailable · 2 removed." and Undo. Toasts pause their countdown while hovered or focused.
- **Connection pill**: under the header. "You're offline…" (with how many changes are waiting), "Reconnecting… live updates are paused", then "Back online · all changes sent". Changes made offline are kept on the phone, even across a reload. An add aimed at a round confirmed meanwhile comes back as "not saved" with **Add to round N**.
- **After Add**: dish, quantity, size and instructions reset; the recipient stays; focus returns to the Dish field; toast "Added Gyoza ×2. Ayos!" with Undo. The line scrolls into view above the composer and gets the saffron wash, also when an existing dish gets another entry (yours or from another phone).
- **Remove** (stepper at 1): no dialog; toast with Undo restores it.
- **Failures**: a red box lists each change that did not save, by name, with Discard (and Retry when a second try can help: server error or too many changes). A conflict ("Someone changed this dish just now") refreshes to the latest version.
- **Bottom bar**: item count for the round and **Show waiter**.

## Views (tabs under the header)
- **Order** (default): the consolidated ticket. **People** (count of joined people): one card per joined person (the host's only when it has dishes), then names the host typed for people who did not join ("added by the host"), then Whole table and Not assigned; guests show "Ready" or "Still choosing". **Groups** (count of group labels): each collective order with its total, dishes, member chips and "Add/Edit members" (a dialog with checkboxes; optional, never assigns dishes). Arrow keys move between tabs.
- **Claims**: in People, a guest sees "That's mine" next to each dish the host added under a typed name or for nobody; after tapping, "Asked the host". The host sees a saffron banner at the top: "Rafa says Halo-halo ×1 is theirs." with Decline / Approve. Approving makes it the guest's own dish.
- **Possibly ordered twice** (host, People): when the host added a dish for a guest by name and that guest also added the same dish preparation, a hint offers "Remove your copy" (with Undo). Nothing merges on its own.
- **Readiness**: guests have an "I'm ready" toggle in the bottom bar (pressed state "Ready"); changing their own dishes afterwards resets it. The host sees "n of m guests ready" under the item count. It never blocks anything.

## Invite sheet (header "Invite")
- Bottom drawer torn off the pad (swipe down, Escape or tap outside to close; focus moves in and returns): QR code (ink on white), the 6-character code in large mono, **Share invite** (system share sheet with the text "Join our order at … on Salo: <link>") when available, **Copy link**, and a note that guests change only their own dishes and only the host confirms with the waiter.

## Invite page (`/j/:code`)
- A ticket with the logo, "Join the order (at <name>)", a live line "Live · n people · n items so far", one sentence on how it works, **Your name** and **Join the order**. Closed orders say "This order has ended". The link preview card shows the same title, "Tap to add your dishes…", and an "Add your order" pill.

## Guests in the workspace
- First visit after joining: a toast "You're in! Add your dishes below. Only you can change yours."
- In People, dishes the host typed under the guest's own name appear inside the guest's card ("The host added these for 'Rafa'. Yours?" with That's mine), not as a second card.
- Same screen; the composer's *For* defaults to **Me** (the host's too). Steppers appear only on the guest's own entries; the order name is not editable. Changes from everyone appear live; "Reconnecting…" shows while the live connection is down.

## Updates (bell and drawer)
- A bell at the right of header row 1 (44px target, accessible name "Updates, N unread", badge "9+" above 9) opens the **Updates** drawer; it never resizes row 2. Unread increases are announced politely and focus is not moved.
- Drawer: "Mark all read" in the header, newest first; unread rows show a dot and the word "New"; each row has a local time. Empty: "No updates yet. You'll see confirmations, changes and served food here." Readable on closed orders.
- Tapping a row marks it read, closes the drawer and goes to the Served section, the Rounds tab, the KKB or the Order tab, with focus on the target.
- Updates: round confirmed or opened, dish unavailable, order corrected (only when told to the waiter, D22), table price changed, food served, everyone ready (host), order ended. Never the person who did it; never routine dish additions.
- Unavailable tab: "Kovie and Miguel get an update." names who is told (nothing if only the host; "Everyone else" above three).

## Review, waiter ticket and confirmation
- Bottom bar: the host has **Review order** (primary); guests have **Ticket**.
- **Review round n** (full screen): checks with icons and words (everything saved / changes still saving / changes not saved; n of m guests ready and who is still choosing; possibly ordered twice with "Remove your copy"), then the round's ticket. **Show the waiter** opens the waiter ticket; **Mark confirmed with waiter** (host, enabled only when everything is saved) asks once; the dialog stays open showing "Confirming…" until the server answers and shows any refusal inside it: "Confirm round n with the waiter? This saves round n exactly as shown… Its dishes can't be changed afterwards." → **Yes, the waiter has it**.
- **Waiter ticket** (full screen, in place so unsaved changes are never lost): huge counts, preparations indented, a separate **Corrections** block, total, Copy as text, screen kept awake; the host can confirm from an outline button under the ticket (the screen belongs to the waiter). Showing it never confirms.
- After confirming: the rubber **CONFIRMED** stamp lands in the ticket's header (the signature moment; static with reduced motion), toast "Round n confirmed. Kain na!". The Order tab shows the stamped ticket and, for the host, **Start round n+1**; guests read that the host confirmed the round.
- **Served (food tracker)**: a section on the Order tab, only once any round is confirmed. With no round open it sits between the stamped ticket and What's next?; while the next round is open it sits above the draft ticket, so round 1's food stays trackable. A polite summary ("9 of 14 served · 5 waiting", or "All 14 served. Kain na!") over a thin progress bar, chips All | Waiting n | Served, then rows grouped under "Round n": dish (with size and notes), "3 of 5 served · 2 waiting" (or "✓ Served 3"), who it is for ("Kovie ×2 · Miguel ×3"), and for the host a − / + stepper (44 px) and **All served**. A told replacement is its own row starting at 0. If a told cancellation drops what is ordered below what was served: "Now 3 of 3 served · 2 were cancelled". Taps show at once and go out as one change about 300 ms after the last; a failed save says so on the row with **Try again**. Undo is a − tap. Guests and ended orders read only. A row that leaves the current filter hands focus to the next row, else the previous one, else the selected chip. **End order** adds an informative line, never a block: "5 items still waiting to be served. You can still end the order."
- **Rounds tab**: newest first; the open round prints on its carbon-copy colour (round 2 canary, round 3+ pink) with the corrections it carries (no Remove there; the Corrections list below has it); each confirmed round is one button with a chevron (`aria-expanded`), wrapping on purpose: "Round 1 · Confirmed 7:42 PM" on line 1, "6 dishes · 14 items" (smaller, ink-soft) on line 2, collapsed by default; expanding shows its stamped ticket, rendered from the immutable snapshot. The open round always shows in full. Each confirmed round (collapsed or not) has a host-only **Change a dish…** button. It opens one drawer with two steps: a list of the round's dish lines ("Chicken Katsu (Large) ×5", fully cancelled lines left out; a **Search dishes** field only from 8 lines, filtering as you type), then the **correction sheet** ("Change Sisig (Large)", "Round 1 · 5 ordered", an "All dishes" back button): Cancel some (a stepper per person, every person starting at 0, note, a live "Cancels: Rafa ×2, Kovie ×1" line above Save, Save disabled until 1 or more; each person's count comes only off that person's own entries, newest first; "Goes to the waiter with round N"), Replace (text, note) and Unavailable ("All N … , every size, come off now", optional "Replace with…" which covers what remains after pending cancels (those become told cancels), Mark unavailable, then a confirm with Not now: "Confirmed dishes come off now. This can't be undone."; Escape closes only the confirm; the toast offers Undo (or Undo removal) for the removed open-round entries only). **Replaced dishes** (the waiter was told): the original keeps a plain history row, "Chicken Katsu (Large) ×1 — Replaced" (not pickable, only the count in mono), and the replacement is its own line directly beneath it, nested with a small left indent and a hidden "↳": "↳ Pork Katsu ×1 — Replacement" (the effective quantity; accessible name "Pork Katsu ×1, replacement for Chicken Katsu (Large)"), grouped by replacement text and by person. The original keeps its place in the list; several replacements of one original follow the order they were told. Searching for a replacement also keeps its history row above it. Picking it opens Cancel some and Replace only, with "Not available? Cancel it instead."; its cancels and re-replacements are written on the same source entries, so the original is never cancelled again, only the newest replacement shows, and billing, food tracking and KKB keep reading the same effective quantities. A replacement not yet told (its round is still open) leaves the original as the active line with "change pending (→ Pork Katsu)" and no replacement line. A replaced original whose replacement is fully cancelled shows only the history row. **Focus is never lost**: if the picked line disappears or reaches 0 while its form is open (after a save or a live update) the sheet goes back to the list and focuses its first line; if nothing is left to change the sheet closes. Closing returns focus to Change a dish…, or to that round's summary button when the round has nothing left to change. Cancel and Replace need an open round; without one the sheet says "Start round N to send changes to the waiter" with a Start round N button that keeps the sheet open on this tab; Unavailable always works. A **Corrections** list closes the tab, one row per change, grouped by source dish (dishes in the order they first changed, oldest change first inside a dish: "Chicken Katsu changed to Pork Katsu", then "Pork Katsu ×1 cancelled"; the live waiter ticket's Corrections block uses the same order, confirmed tickets stay as stored): "Sisig ×1 cancelled · Unavailable · Told the waiter 7:50 PM" or "… −2 · Pending · goes with round 2" with Remove (the whole change). **End order** (host) asks once and explains: nobody can change anything, the invite stops working, tickets stay readable for 7 days, then deleted; warns if the open round was never confirmed.
- **Who ordered what** has two tabs, **Items** and **KKB**. Items (no prices, as below) is unchanged; KKB is described after it.
- **Items**: opens by itself for the host after End order, as a button on the ended order for everyone, and on the People tab once a round is confirmed. Per person, then groups ("shared by …"), the whole table and unassigned dishes, from confirmed rounds only with corrections applied (cancellations lower counts; a swap reads "A → B"); an unconfirmed round is mentioned and left out. **Copy text** (plain text for the group chat), **Save image** (a PNG ticket: the share sheet on phones, a download elsewhere), **Share text** where supported. The date is the device's own. Items follows KKB on corrections: only cancellations and swaps the waiter was told about (their round confirmed) change the counts. A dish with a change still waiting for a round keeps its full count and shows "waiting for the waiter"; the Copy text adds "Changes waiting for the waiter: …" only then.
- **KKB tab** (reachable once there are dishes: People banner, the host's What's next? → *Enter receipt prices (KKB)*, Rounds → *KKB & service charge*, the ended order's button; End order opens it on KKB when any price is set). A completeness line first, always: "8 of 10 dishes priced · Show the 2 without a price" (dishes = distinct dish + size + notes lines; priced only when every entry resolves, table price included; the link opens that person's card and focuses the first "Add price") or "All prices in"; then a **Copy summary** button (everyone; plus Share summary where supported; "Summary copied" shows next to it) whose plain text comes from `kkbText` and agrees with the view (name and date, one line per person, "Not yet settled:", "Total ₱X" or "Total not final: …", "Made with Salo"); Items copy and Save image stay price-free. Then the status line (service charge not entered / split by food / not yet allocated · n things to review, which jumps to the reasons). The host's name prompt while they are still "Host". Then the viewer's own card (lines with price each and subtotal, Food or Priced subtotal, and, once allocated, Service charge and **Total with service charge**), everyone else as collapsible cards (the host's cards with missing prices start open), then Shared · settle at the table (groups, Whole table, Not assigned: "₱X shared" or "No prices yet", Billed to …). Each line's **Add price** / amount opens an inline editor: Price each (₱) with the line subtotal, and for the host on confirmed shared dishes **Bill to** chips (No one yet, each person, typed names). Saving waits for the server; on failure the amount stays with the reason and **Try again** (same request, applied once); if someone else changed it first: "Someone changed this just now to ₱X" with Use theirs / Keep mine. **Table prices**: a line shows its source, "₱120.00 each · table price" when it follows the table price for its dish + size (notes ignored), "₱100.00 each · this line only" when it has its own price and a table price exists, otherwise just "₱X each". For the host on a dish line the editor first offers **Every <Dish (Size)> here (n lines)** (default; n counts lines that will follow it: no price of their own, all rounds, replacements excluded) or **Only this line**. Every sets the table price and clears this line's own price in the same save; it never overwrites other lines' own prices; a blank price removes the table price (button *Remove table price*). Only this line is the per-line price as before. Guests see table prices read-only and their own dishes' prices stay overrides; clearing a line's price makes it follow the table price. Replacement lines never inherit one. Last, **Service charge**: the host types it from the receipt (Blank = not entered · 0 = none), guests read it; when it can't be split yet the reasons are listed (items without a price and whose, shared dishes with no payer, changes pending with the waiter, nothing priced). Ended orders show the same, read-only. Prices never appear on the waiter ticket, its copy text, the invite or its preview, or the Items copy/image.
- **Check against the receipt** (KKB, under Service charge, visible to everyone): Food in Salo, Service charge ("not entered" when blank), Salo total, Receipt total (host types it, guests read it). One line says "₱36.00 more on the receipt. Not split. Check the receipt for items missing in Salo.", "₱20.00 less on the receipt…" or "Matches the receipt."; with unpriced dishes it says "2 dishes have no price yet, so this can't match." instead. The difference is never distributed. Read-only after the order ends.
- **Host name**: starting an order stays one tap, so the host begins as "Host". Right away, at the top of the order, a saffron card asks "What should the table call you?" (Save / Later; Later hides it until the order is opened again). It stays until a name is given. The name can also be set in Order options (**Your name**), on step 1 of the setup guide, and from the KKB tab.
- **Ended order**: banner "This order has ended… deleted on <weekday, date>", Rounds tab by default, no composer, no Invite.

## Waiter ticket link (`/s/:id/waiter`)
- Large dish names and counts (fluid type), preparations indented, total. Works read aloud or handed over; the screen is kept awake while open (where supported).
- **Copy as text** produces the same ticket as plain text. Viewing never confirms or sends anything.

## Not part of the order / deleted (`404`)
- "Wala dito." with an explanation that orders are deleted a week after the meal, and **Start a new order**.

## Slice 8: UX polish (composer, dock, Served, round labels, My share, viewport)
- **Composer states**: at rest (empty dish, no focus inside) the composer is the Dish field and one row, **For ▾ · Add**. Focus inside, or any typed text, quantity above 1 or filled detail, expands it: the Add row gains the stepper and the Size / Note / ₱ Price chips sit on their own row below it. Under 360 px the chips hide behind a **+ Details** toggle (`aria-expanded`; name "Details, 2 added" once filled). Filled details stay as removable ✕ tags. A tap on a button never expands the composer by itself, so a layout shift cannot swallow the tap; a basic dish is Dish then Add with no extra tap.
- **Dock while typing (D27)**: the bottom bar (count, Review order, Ticket, I'm ready, My share) is hidden while focus is inside the composer and returns on blur, so the keyboard plus composer fit a 320 px phone. The Undo toast stacks above the dock through `--dock-h`, so it is not lost. `html` `scroll-padding-bottom` follows `--dock-h`.
- **Served (D24)**: one **Waiting** group (nothing or part served), then **Fully served (N)** across all rounds in stable order, bucketed on the count the server holds. Every row carries a `.round-label` tag. Fully served rows are compact (dish, ×N, All ✓, names); the host taps one (`aria-expanded`) to reveal − / + and Change…; guests get a plain row. The group is a native `<details>` that starts closed above 5 dishes. Replaced-dish history rows live in Fully served. The filter chips are gone; the last-resort focus target is the "Served" heading. After an undo moves a dish back to Waiting, focus follows its − control and scrolls it into view.
- **Round labels**: one `.round-label` (DM Mono 500, uppercase in CSS, 0.14em tracking) on the ticket and waiter headings, the header status, the Served tags and KKB's draft heading; the DOM text stays "Round N". "Not yet confirmed" reads "ROUND N · NOT YET CONFIRMED" once the number is known. An expanded Rounds card hides the duplicate sans "Round N" from sight (still in its accessible name).
- **My share (D26)**: once a round is confirmed, guests and the host get a dock button. Labels: "My share · ₱528" (allocated), "My share · ₱480" (complete, no charge yet), "My share so far · ₱480" (partial or pending), "My share · no prices yet"; no confirmed dishes, no button. Whole pesos. One tap opens Who ordered what on KKB, then scrolls to and focuses the viewer's own card once the drawer has opened. On an ended order "Who ordered what" opens the same way. Under 360 px the count keeps its own line and the buttons wrap below it.
- **Viewport and native feel (D1)**: `interactive-widget=resizes-content` so the layout viewport shrinks with the keyboard (the dock stays above it). MDN browser-compat-data: Chrome Android 108, Firefox Android 133, Safari 27.3 (iOS mirrors Safari); older iOS ignores it, so iOS before 27.3 keeps the keyboard overlaying the page and relies on `scroll-padding-bottom` and the hidden bottom bar. Side effects checked: the recipient panel (`max-h-[45dvh]`) and drawers (`max-h-[92dvh]`) shrink with the keyboard and scroll inside, which is wanted; `min-h-dvh` shells just follow the smaller viewport. The session page sets a dark `theme-color` for dark mode; buttons use `-webkit-touch-callout: none; user-select: none`. A WebKit (iPhone) Playwright project runs the standalone, composer-details, kkb and food-tracker specs.

## Accessibility
- 44 px targets, visible focus rings, labelled controls (stepper buttons name the dish and who it is for), status in words and icons, toasts in a polite live region that never takes focus, reduced motion respected, light and dark checked with axe (WCAG 2.2 AA tags) in the end-to-end tests.

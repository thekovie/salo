Scope: whole app shell (Operate) — workspace /s/[id], review, waiter ticket, join, invite sheet; landing / is Persuade-lite (tool first, short how-it-works below).
Audience/job: host collecting a table's order one-handed while the waiter waits; guests adding their own dishes.
Constraints: brief-pinned "Order ticket" world; bottom dock, no sidebar; light weights only; WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Salo is the order pad itself, made live. Refuses the generic SaaS card list on grey: every order is a ticket strip with a torn perforated edge, quantities set big in mono like a cook's tally.
OWN-WORLD: Carbonless order-pad materials, full range: thermal paper ground, tomato-red ink for numbers and the primary action, ballpoint blue-black text, canary-yellow and pink carbon-copy sheets for later rounds, saffron for highlights. Fredoka 500 for headings, Nunito Sans for body, DM Mono for quantities and ticket numbers. Dark mode = the same pad under a dim bar light: warm charcoal, ink inverted, red kept.
STORY: Start → type a dish → it lands on the ticket with its count → friends add theirs live → review → show the ticket → stamp it confirmed → next round prints on a canary copy.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Workspace: tomato header band (session name, Round chip, sync pill, Invite); ticket strip filling the middle with dish lines and big right-aligned counts; composer pinned above the bottom bar (dish field, stepper, Add); bottom bar with total items and "Review order".
FORM: Brief-pinned direction (no concept roll; owner chose "Order ticket" 2026-11-06). Signature move: confirming drops a red rubber "CONFIRMED" stamp on the round's ticket, and each new round is a new carbon-copy colour.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
